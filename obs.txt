ROI CROPPING LOGIC: 
- max priority is largest area connected blob
- second priority is spatial clusters of small connected components 
- last priority is all foreground images in a single ROI 


A reasonable MVP target would be:
- 500–1000 positive ROIs
- 500–1000 negative ROIs

Negatives should include:
- weave texture
- lighting variation
- shadows
- wrinkles
- harmless texture changes
- every kind of false alarm Layer 1 produces

10–20% overlap across some samples: not a concern.
70–80% of the dataset being consecutive frames of the same defect: worth avoiding.
